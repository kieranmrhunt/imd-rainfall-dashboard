Source: India Meteorological Department, 0.25 x 0.25 degree daily gridded rainfall binary archive. Grid is 135 x 129 points from 6.5N, 66.5E to 38.5N, 100.0E; units are mm.
Boundaries: geoBoundaries India ADM0 and ADM1 geometries, simplified for browser rendering.
Citation: Pai D.S., Latha Sridhar, Rajeevan M., Sreejith O.P., Satbhai N.S. and Mukhopadhyay B. (2014), MAUSAM, 65, 1, pp. 1-18.
Method: region totals are area-weighted with cosine-latitude weights over valid IMD grid cells. State and union territory boundaries use geoBoundaries ADM1 geometries. Day rankings compare against the selected region's baseline mean daily rainfall for that calendar month; month rankings compare against the baseline monthly mean. Percentage anomaly is 100 x (rainfall - normal) / normal.
